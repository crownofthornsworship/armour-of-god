# The Armour of God — Rebuild Roadmap

## Product goal

Turn the current six-mission collection into a clear action-adventure where each piece of God's armour changes how the player moves, protects others, and solves problems. The game needs one understandable core loop before more content is added.

## Core loop

Explore a compact mission, recognize the challenge, equip the appropriate armour, use its unique ability, protect someone or complete an objective, then return to the hub with a visible upgrade.

## Phase 1 — Concept and controls

- Audit all six missions and remove interactions that do not support the core loop.
- Build a short playable tutorial around the Belt of Truth.
- Standardize movement, interaction, attack/ability, pause, and touch controls.
- Add clear objectives, wayfinding, checkpoints, damage rules, and retry behavior.

## Phase 2 — Six meaningful armour abilities

- Belt of Truth: reveal disguises, hidden paths, and false statements.
- Breastplate of Righteousness: guard vulnerable characters and absorb a limited hit.
- Shoes of Peace: cross unstable ground and create temporary safe paths.
- Shield of Faith: directional blocking, projectile reflection, and ally cover.
- Helmet of Salvation: resist fear/confusion effects and reveal the correct route.
- Sword of the Spirit: active melee combat against shadows plus Scripture-based finishing choices.

## Phase 3 — Presentation

- Create a consistent hero animation set with equipped-armour variants.
- Replace generic rooms and effects with illustrated mission environments and readable enemies.
- Animate every ability at the character, not as a disconnected overlay.
- Add an armour-room hub that visually fills as missions are completed.

## Phase 4 — Campaign

- Rebuild six missions so each teaches one piece and later missions combine them.
- Add optional rescues, hidden Scripture seals, time-free mastery goals, and a final multi-ability boss.
- Add a mission map, completion grades, replay, and persistent progress.

## Release gate

- Full campaign playthrough on keyboard, controller-like touch controls, portrait, landscape, and desktop.
- Verify readable Scripture references, accessibility options, saves, reset, pause, and no dead-end states.
- Refresh social preview, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio.
